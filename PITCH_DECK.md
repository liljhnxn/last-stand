### Slide 1: Title & Vision
* **Title:** LastStand
* **Subtitle:** The Premier On-Chain Survival Gauntlet on Botchain
* **Tagline:** One Life. One Chance. How Long Can You Survive?
* **Summary:** A lightweight, high-adrenaline rogue-lite mini-game running 100% on-chain and in-browser with zero backend servers.

---

### Slide 2: The Problem
* **Web3 Games Are Too Bloated:** Multi-gigabyte downloads, complicated onboarding, and high gas friction prevent casual engagement.
* **Fake "Decentralization":** 95% of current games store critical logic and leaderboards on centralized AWS/GCP servers.
* **Network Inactivity:** Chains need fast, engaging, sticky dApps that drive repeat on-chain transactions and active wallet metrics.

---

### Slide 3: The Solution — LastStand
* **Zero Barrier to Entry:** Instant play directly from any Web3 browser or MetaMask wallet.
* **100% Serverless:** 1 single-page client + 1 Solidity contract on Botchain. No central servers, no downtime.
* **Addictive Push-Your-Luck Loop:** 10 rounds of escalating danger where risk equals glory.

---

### Slide 4: Gameplay Mechanics
* **10 Lethal Encounters:** Every round ramps up risk.
  * 🟢 **Safe:** 95% survival rate
  * 🟡 **Danger:** 75% survival rate
  * 🔴 **Critical:** 50% survival rate (late game coin-flip)
* **The Core Choice:**
  * **CONTINUE:** Risk everything for +1 point and higher standing.
  * **EVACUATE:** Cash out early to preserve your score.
* **Permanent Inscription:** Players permanently stamp their survival record on Botchain Mainnet.

---

### Slide 5: Technical Architecture
* **Frontend:** Next.js 14 App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS.
* **Web3 Stack:** Wagmi v2, Viem, TanStack Query.
* **Blockchain Core:** Solidity `^0.8.20` deployed on Botchain Mainnet (`Chain ID: 677`).
* **Contract Address:** `0x59fd31fA96fdEA0aeb85F5E541Ab47bB8f91012a`.
* **Zero Backend Costs:** Operates entirely client-side and smart-contract-side.
